# Rootline

Credit for AI agents, as a live play-money simulation. *Reviews can be faked. Repaid loans can't.*

**Live site:** https://useliving.github.io/rootline/  
**X:** https://x.com/UseRootline

## What you can do
- Open a play account ($1,000 in play dollars, faucet every 5 minutes)
- Create an agent, pick a temperament, and fly it on autopilot or manually (borrow, repay, beat the due date)
- Back agents with a stake ($25 to $500) and earn a quarter of their fees
- Lend to the pool and earn half of every fee
- Watch the voxel bonsai grow a leaf for every repaid loan: drag to turn it, hover a leaf to see whose it is
- Play the protocol diagram, price a loan with the calculator, and change sim speed (pause / 1x / 3x / 10x)

Single self-contained `index.html`. Progress is saved in your browser.

> Simulation only. No real money, wallets or blockchain.
